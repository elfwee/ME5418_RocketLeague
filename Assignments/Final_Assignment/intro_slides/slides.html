Dueling Double DQN

Off-policy · value-based
Heads: V(s) + A(s,a) → Q(s,a)
acts greedily on the best Q
  • 100k-step replay buffer, Huber loss
  • Double-DQN targets, periodic target sync
  • ε-greedy exploration, 1.0 → 0.05

PPO Actor–Critic

On-policy · policy gradient
Heads: policy π(a|s) + critic V(s)
samples while training, greedy at test
  • 4,096-step rollouts, 6 update epochs
  • GAE (λ = 0.95), clipped objective (ε = 0.2)
  • Early stop when KL divergence > 0.02

04 · Lessons learnedME5418 · Group 41

The agents found loopholes. We closed them.

01
Parked as a ramp
Pitched its nose into the floor so rolling balls rode up the car and into the net.
Fix

Rebuilt the chassis with a vertical front bumper.

02
Farmed the corner
Nudged the ball up a curved corner wall over and over to collect "progress" reward.
Fix

Progress reward is weighted by ball height, so floor rolls earn far less.

03
Waited for mistakes
Hung back and let the scripted bot knock the ball into its own goal.
Fix

A goal the opponent touched last pays +2, not +20.

04
Camped in goal
Sat in its own goal mouth to avoid conceding and never crossed midfield.
Fix

A small penalty every step for distance to the ball.

Every fix went into the physics or the reward, not the learning algorithm.
